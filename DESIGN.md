## Brand & Style
This design system embodies the intersection of high-precision Japanese transit choreography and ultra-refined fintech expense tracking. Inspired by the deep abyss of the Pacific trenches meeting the crisp, alpine clarity of the Northern Alps, the aesthetic delivers an atmosphere of calm mastery, hyper-accurate telemetry, and high-tech luxury. 

The visual style blends **Modern Japanese Editorial** with **Glacial Glassmorphism** and structured technical data displays. The interface relies on luminous gradient flows, crisp optical glass cards, fine hairline boundaries, and expansive negative space. It rejects cluttered utilitarian travel dashboards in favor of an ethereal, high-end travel atelier: dark mode mimics night transit across neon-lit Shinkansen tracks and oceanic mist, while light mode evokes pristine winter mornings in Hokkaido over glazed porcelain architecture. Every interaction must feel frictionless, deliberate, and mathematically balanced.

## Colors
The color architecture captures the transition from oceanic midnight depth to glacial refraction. The system defaults to dark mode for an immersive, luminescent travel experience, supported by a mirrored, ultra-clean light mode.

### Dark Mode Palette (Default)
- **Base Canvas (Abyss Midnight):** `#040914` blending subtly into `#081326` for surface depth.
- **Primary Gradient (Deep Sapphire to Vivid Cobalt):** `#1d4ed8` to `#2563eb`. Used for primary CTAs, journey progress paths, and active exchange triggers.
- **Accents & Telemetry (Arctic Glacial Cyan):** `#38bdf8` and `#06b6d4`. Reserved for live Shinkansen status indicators, IC card balance alerts, currency conversion rate spikes, and specular frosted edge highlights.
- **Typography & Surfaces:** Primary text in Ice White (`#f8fafc`); secondary and metadata in Cool Slate (`#94a3b8`); surface overlays use semi-transparent glacial navy tints (`rgba(8, 19, 38, 0.7)`).

### Light Mode Palette (Cool Pearl & Ice Mist)
- **Base Canvas:** Soft glacial gradient from `#f0f4fa` to `#f8faff`.
- **Surfaces & Cards:** Crisp optical white (`#ffffff`) layered with hairline royal indigo borders (`#dbeafe` to `#bfdbfe`).
- **Primary Interactive Elements:** Royal Indigo (`#1e3a8a`) flowing into Electric Blue (`#2563eb`).
- **Typography:** Deep Charcoal Navy (`#0f172a`) for maximum legibility and tactile luxury, with Slate Indigo (`#475569`) for subtext.

## Typography
The typographic voice pairs the literary poise of classical Japanese editorial publication with the uncompromising accuracy of financial infrastructure.

- **Editorial Headings (Noto Serif):** Used exclusively for narrative milestones, destination headings, station departures, and high-level expenditure summaries. Evokes classical restraint, prestige, and mindful travel.
- **Interface & System Content (Manrope):** Clean, geometric-humanist balance designed for high legibility across dense travel itineraries, merchant categories, and navigational tags.
- **Financial & Transit Telemetry (JetBrains Mono):** Mandatory for foreign exchange conversions (JPY/USD/EUR), Pasmo/Suica balances, real-time platform tracking, Shinkansen car/seat metrics, and departure timestamps. Always enforce `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to guarantee vertical numeric alignment across rapid conversions.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a flexible, mathematical column grid system inspired by traditional Japanese proportional spacing (*Ma*), modified for data-dense fintech displays.

- **Grid Architecture:** 4 columns on mobile (&lt;768px), 8 columns on tablet (768px–1024px), and 12 columns on desktop (&gt;1024px) capped at a max-width of 1360px for analytical clarity.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Multiples of 4px and 8px govern all component spacing. Generous section gaps (`space-xl`) separate high-level journey phases, while internal data pairs (e.g., JPY amount to home currency conversion) maintain tight cohesion (`space-xs` to `space-sm`).
- **Responsive Adaptations:** On handheld screens, financial telemetry cards transform into horizontally scrolling edge-to-edge panes with snap anchors, while desktop interfaces present split-screen choreography: transit live status and route topology on the left, running ledger and category analytics on the right.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is rendered strictly through tonal stratification, frosted refraction, and glacial rim illumination—avoiding muddy, drop-heavy cast shadows.

- **Ground Plane (Level 0):** Deep abyssal canvas with radial gradients radiating from the primary interactive focal points.
- **Glacial Glassmorphic Cards (Level 1):** Background `rgba(8, 19, 38, 0.65)` in dark mode (or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` in light mode), accompanied by a `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(180%)`. Outlined with a razor-thin 1px border using `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.15)` at the top gradient fading into `rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.05)` at the base.
- **Hover & Interaction (Level 2):** Elements rise with an arctic cyan specular rim highlight (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.35)`) and an ultra-diffused atmospheric back-glow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.12)`.
- **Modals, Floating Ledgers & Sheets (Level 3):** High-density optical shield with `backdrop-filter: blur(28px)`, framed by an inner glow `inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` and a soft oceanic shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(4, 9, 20, 0.6)`.

## Shapes
A roundedness tier of 2 provides structural precision with soft, organic aerodynamic curves reminiscent of Shinkansen nose cones and polished sea glass.

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** `0.5rem` (8px) radius. Crisp enough to feel architectural and rigorous, soft enough to invite touch.
- **Glacial Cards & Panels:** `1rem` (16px) radius for major modular cards (e.g., Daily Budget Tracker, Station Waypoint summary).
- **Floating Modals & IC Cards:** `1.5rem` (24px) radius for luxury focal pieces, tactile payment overlays, and route timeline sheets.
- **Pill Accents:** Specific pill-shaped radiuses (`9999px`) are restricted solely to real-time status chips (e.g., "ON TIME", "CONVERTED", "TAX-FREE ELIGIBLE").

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Journey/Log Expense):** Vibrant horizontal gradient spanning Sapphire (`#1d4ed8`) to Vivid Cobalt (`#2563eb`), top-lit by a faint 1px white ice highlight. Hover triggers a subtle Glacial Cyan bloom.
- **Secondary / Technical Action:** Transparent glass backdrop with a 1px border in `#38bdf8` at 30% opacity, white text, and instant cyan glow on interaction.
- **Destructive / Cancellation:** Transparent tinted crimson (`rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.1)`) with a hairline border in `#ef4444`.

### Cards & Ledger Panes
- Layered surfaces featuring frosted indigo-tinted glass. 
- Headers feature classical serif naming (e.g., *Kyoto Shinkansen Nozomi 43*) accompanied by JetBrains Mono live telemetry on the right (e.g., `¥13,320 · $89.40 USD`).
- Card separators use hairline gradient dividers: `linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25), transparent)`.

### Input Fields & Conversion Calculators
- Input boxes employ recessed abyssal backdrops (`rgba(4, 9, 20, 0.8)`) with recessed top inner shadows.
- Active focus smoothly shifts border color to Arctic Glacial Cyan (`#06b6d4`) with a soft 4px exterior glow ring.
- Numeric inputs automatically surface the currency ticker (`JPY`, `USD`, `EUR`) in monospaced format with real-time live rate updates right-aligned.

### Chips & Transit Tags
- Micro-pills rendered with `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.12)` backgrounds and crisp text in `#38bdf8`.
- Transit line pills mirror the official Japan railway colors (e.g., Yamanote Green, Marunouchi Red) encased within dark glacial borders to maintain aesthetic unity.

### Checkboxes, Switches & Radio Controls
- Custom controls styled as machined glass toggles. 
- Activated state fills with vivid cobalt, housing a pure ice white indicator dot that glows with subtle glacial cyan ambience.

### Specialized Travel & Fintech Components
- **Suica/Pasmo Live Gauge:** A virtual contactless card module displaying dynamic balance, tap-in/tap-out transit gates, and instant JPY-to-home-currency auto-top-up thresholds.
- **Route Chrono-Timeline:** A vertical glowing line anchored by Arctic Glacial nodes representing transfer stations, displaying departure countdowns in monospaced tabular numerals.
- **Daily Budget Compass:** A circular progress arc shaded with an oceanic-to-cyan gradient revealing spend ratios across dining, transit, accommodation, and duty-free shopping.
